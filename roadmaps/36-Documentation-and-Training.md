# 1. PHASE PURPOSE

The Documentation & Training framework establishes a comprehensive enterprise approach for documenting, maintaining, sharing, and transferring knowledge throughout the SITADC Youth Hub.

Its purpose is to ensure that every stakeholder—including administrators, developers, directors, coordinators, volunteers, partners, and end users—has access to accurate, consistent, and up-to-date information required to effectively use, administer, support, maintain, and enhance the platform.

Documentation shall become an integral part of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), while training shall ensure organizational capacity is continuously strengthened.
