# 1. PHASE PURPOSE

The Finance and Resource Mobilization module shall provide a centralized, secure, transparent, and enterprise-grade financial management system for the SITADC Youth Hub.

The module shall enable the organization to effectively plan, receive, manage, allocate, monitor, report, and audit financial resources while strengthening sustainability through grants, fundraising, partnerships, sponsorships, donor engagement, and other resource mobilization initiatives.
