# 1. PHASE PURPOSE

The Stakeholder Management module shall serve as the official stakeholder registry and relationship management system for the SITADC Youth Organization.

It shall provide a centralized platform for identifying, registering, engaging, collaborating with, monitoring, evaluating, and maintaining relationships with stakeholders while promoting transparency, accountability, sustainable partnerships, and organizational growth.

The module shall become the authoritative source for all stakeholder information across the organization.
