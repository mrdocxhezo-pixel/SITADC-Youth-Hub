# 1. PHASE PURPOSE

The Testing & Quality Assurance (QA) framework establishes a comprehensive enterprise testing strategy for the SITADC Youth Hub.

Its purpose is to ensure every component of the application is functionally correct, secure, reliable, performant, accessible, maintainable, and production-ready before release.

Testing shall be embedded throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), ensuring defects are identified and resolved as early as possible.

The framework shall support continuous integration, continuous testing, continuous validation, and continuous quality improvement.

The objective is to provide measurable confidence that every SITADC Youth Hub release satisfies organizational requirements and quality expectations.
