# 1. PHASE PURPOSE

The Export Engine shall provide a centralized, secure, configurable, and enterprise-grade document generation service for the SITADC Youth Hub.

The module shall enable authorized users to generate professional exports from every approved module while preserving organizational branding, document integrity, confidentiality, digital verification, approval history, and auditability.

The Export Engine shall become the official export service for all organizational reports, registers, dashboards, documents, analytics, and structured records.

* Export governance

* Export principles
* Export lifecycle
* Supported export formats
* Branding standards

* Document standardization

The module shall provide a consistent export experience throughout the SITADC Youth Hub.
