# 1. PHASE PURPOSE

The Review and Approval module shall provide a structured, secure, configurable, and auditable workflow for reviewing, validating, commenting on, approving, rejecting, returning, escalating, and digitally signing reports submitted through the Report Management module.

The module shall ensure that every organizational report is reviewed according to SITADC Youth Organization governance policies before becoming an approved organizational record.

---

This phase establishes:

* Review governance
* Approval hierarchy
* Review lifecycle
* Review architecture
* Reviewer responsibilities
* Decision framework
* Comment framework
* Review status framework
* Digital approval standards
* Dashboard overview

The module shall become the organization's centralized review and approval system.
