# 1. PHASE PURPOSE

The Volunteer Management module shall serve as the official volunteer registry and coordination system for the SITADC Youth Organization.

It shall provide a centralized platform for recruiting, registering, onboarding, deploying, supervising, supporting, evaluating, recognizing, and retaining volunteers while ensuring accountability, safeguarding, operational efficiency, and measurable community impact.

The module shall become the authoritative source for all volunteer information across the organization.
