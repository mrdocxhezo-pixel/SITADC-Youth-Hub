# 1. PHASE PURPOSE

The Organizational Registers module shall provide a centralized, secure, configurable, and enterprise-grade register management system for maintaining all official organizational registers within the SITADC Youth Hub.

The module shall provide standardized methods for recording, validating, maintaining, tracking, reviewing, approving, archiving, restoring, and reporting organizational records while preserving institutional memory, accountability, transparency, and governance.

Every register shall become the official source of truth for its respective organizational records.
