# 1. PHASE PURPOSE

The Communication and Media module shall provide a centralized, secure, scalable, and enterprise-grade communication platform for the SITADC Youth Hub.

The module shall enable the organization to plan, create, review, approve, publish, distribute, monitor, and archive all internal and external communications while maintaining consistent branding, improving stakeholder engagement, and supporting evidence-based storytelling.

The module shall serve as the official communication and media management platform for the organization.
