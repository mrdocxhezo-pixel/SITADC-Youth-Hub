# 1. PHASE PURPOSE

The Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning (MEAL) module shall serve as the official organizational system for planning, monitoring, measuring, evaluating, verifying, learning from, and reporting the effectiveness, efficiency, accountability, and impact of all programs, projects, activities, services, and strategic initiatives implemented by the SITADC Youth Organization.

The module shall provide a centralized, secure, configurable, evidence-based platform for managing organizational performance while supporting transparency, accountability, continuous learning, adaptive management, and informed decision-making.

The MEAL module shall become the primary organizational source for measuring outputs, outcomes, impacts, and institutional performance.
