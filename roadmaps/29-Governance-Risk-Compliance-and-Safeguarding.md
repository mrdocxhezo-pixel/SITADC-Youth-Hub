# 1. PHASE PURPOSE

The Governance, Risk, Compliance and Safeguarding (GRCS) module shall provide a centralized, secure, transparent, and enterprise-grade governance and assurance platform for the SITADC Youth Hub.

The module shall enable the organization to establish effective governance structures, identify and manage organizational risks, monitor compliance obligations, uphold ethical standards, protect beneficiaries and stakeholders through safeguarding, and strengthen institutional accountability.

The module shall serve as the official governance and assurance framework for the organization.
