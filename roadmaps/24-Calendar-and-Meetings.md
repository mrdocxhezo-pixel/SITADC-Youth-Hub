# 1. PHASE PURPOSE

The Calendar and Meetings module shall provide a centralized, secure, configurable, and enterprise-grade scheduling and collaboration system for managing organizational calendars, meetings, events, appointments, governance sessions, program activities, reminders, attendance, agendas, minutes, action items, and organizational decisions.

The module shall ensure that all scheduled organizational activities are coordinated, documented, monitored, and integrated with the broader SITADC Youth Hub ecosystem.

---

This phase establishes:

* Calendar governance
* Meeting management standards
* Scheduling framework
* Calendar architecture
* Meeting lifecycle
* Calendar categories
* Meeting types
* Ownership framework
* Metadata standards
* Dashboard overview

The module shall become the official organizational scheduling and meeting management platform.
