# 1. PHASE PURPOSE

The Program & Project Management module shall serve as the official planning, implementation, coordination, monitoring, reporting, and performance management system for all programs and projects undertaken by the SITADC Youth Organization.

The module shall provide a centralized platform for managing strategic initiatives from concept development through implementation, monitoring, evaluation, closure, and organizational learning.

It shall become the authoritative source for all program and project information across the organization.
