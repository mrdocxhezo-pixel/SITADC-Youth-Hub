Use this form to create or update a grant for your organization.

Fields:
  • Name: The name of the grant
  • Grant Number: The unique identifier for the grant
  • Funding Agency: The organization providing the grant
  • Grant Type: The type of grant (e.g., government, foundation)
  • Amount Awarded: The total amount of the grant
  • Currency: The currency of the grant amount
  • Award Date: The date the grant was awarded
  • Start Date: The date the grant period begins
  • End Date: The date the grant period ends
  • Status: The current status of the grant
  • Programme: The programme associated with the grant
  • Project: The project associated with the grant
  • Reporting Frequency: How often reports are required
  • Next Report Due: The date the next report is due
  • Special Conditions: Any special conditions attached to the grant
  • Active: Whether the grant is active
